Late roll call & the audit trail

A foreman who opens roll call after the shift has started cannot record a real tap-in time. The app credits the 07:00 shift start, states that plainly before he confirms, and stamps every resulting record with FOREMAN_LATE_OVERRIDE so HR sees exactly what was assumed and by whom.

The flag is a literal string
FOREMAN_LATE_OVERRIDE is shown in monospace, unchanged, on both the phone and the web. The foreman and the auditor read the same token.
Group by event, not by row
One late roll call is one decision. HR approves 37 records at once, and the exceptions inside it are pulled out by name.
Show the evidence beside the claim
Reason, device offline duration and the independent gate log sit in one row, so HR is not deciding on the foreman's word alone.
Hours carry a peso figure
96.5 hours ≈ ₱78,400. Naming the amount sets the seriousness of the approval without a warning dialog.
