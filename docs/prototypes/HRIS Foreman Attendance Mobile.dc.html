02 — Field rules

Three buttons, always visible
64px tall, full width of the card, 10px apart. No swipe gestures and no long-press — a gloved thumb cannot do either reliably.
The tap writes a timestamp
Recorded on the card in plain words: "Marked present 07:10", with Undo beside it at full 44px. The foreman never types a time on the main path.
Two banners, never merged
Grey states the connection; orange states the queue. Connection and pending work are different facts and a foreman acts on them differently.
Filled means chosen
The selected status fills solid with a glyph; unchosen options drop to grey outline. Readable at arm's length in full sun.
