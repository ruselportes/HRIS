02 — Form rules

Two types, one form
The toggle changes the middle of the form only. Type, subject, reason, photo and the approval chain stay in the same place, so muscle memory carries over.
Presets before pickers
Tonight · Tomorrow · Pick date. 1 day · 2 days · Half day. A calendar exists but is the third choice, not the first.
The cost is shown before sending
Overtime states the peso effect and which multipliers apply. A foreman should know what he is asking for before the engineer does.
Approval chain is named, not numbered
“G. Tabotabo, usually same day” rather than “Tier 1”. The confirmation ends by saying no follow-up is needed.
