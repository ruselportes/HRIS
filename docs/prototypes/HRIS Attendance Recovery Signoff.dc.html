Attendance recovery & sign-off

When a foreman is absent or a device stays offline past the cut-off, the day's attendance arrives incomplete. Recovery reconstructs it from the evidence that does exist — gate biometrics, the acting foreman's notes, the crew manifest — and no reconstructed day reaches payroll without two named signatures.
